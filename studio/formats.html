The Art of Print: Formats & Framing

Every photograph carries its own weight, light, and presence. Choosing the right scale changes how an image breathes in a room and how you connect with it. All prints are produced keeping the native 2:3 aspect ratio, ensuring no cropping or loss of the original composition.

📐 Available Formats

1. 20×30 cm (approx. 8×12″) — Intimacy & Detail

The ideal balance of tactile intimacy and gallery presentation.

  • The Experience: Designed to be held and collected. It offers a deeply personal, tactile connection—large enough to reveal every fine detail, yet light and comfortable in your hands.
  • Framing Solution: Fits seamlessly into standard 30×40 cm frames with a mat (passe-partout), creating a classic, breathing gallery look for any space.
  • Best For: Personal collections, desktop displays, or archival print boxes.

2. 30×45 cm (approx. 12×18″) — The Classic Gallery Standard

The quintessential step into interior display, offering a striking visual presence without overwhelming the room.

  • The Experience: This size commands attention while maintaining a sense of quiet elegance. It is the perfect entry point for hanging photography as a focal point in smaller spaces.
  • Framing Solution: Perfectly pairs with standard 40×50 cm or 40×60 cm frames. Using a wide mat enhances its cinematic depth.
  • Best For: Home offices, cozy reading nooks, or as part of a curated gallery wall.

3. 40×60 cm (approx. 16×24″) — The Living Space Accent

A bold statement size that opens up landscapes and brings architectural lines to life.

  • The Experience: At this scale, the texture of the paper and the depth of the shadows create an immersive window effect. It transforms a blank wall into an intentional architectural element.
  • Framing Solution: Designed to fit standard 50×70 cm frames with a generous mat, or 40×60 cm frames for a clean, borderless, modern look.
  • Best For: Living rooms, bedrooms, or welcoming entryways.

4. 60×90 cm (approx. 24×36″) — Exhibition Scale

An uncompromising, large-format presence that brings the full atmosphere of the moment into your environment.

  • The Experience: Truly immersive. This size is less about observing an image and more about stepping inside it. Every detail becomes a tactile texture on the wall.
  • Framing Solution: Complements standard 70×100 cm exhibition frames with a mat, or looks breathtaking when mounted directly onto a rigid aluminum backing (Dibond) without a frame.
  • Best For: Large accent walls, open-space interiors, or dedicated exhibition spaces.

🌟 Custom Dimensions

Tailored to Your Space

If your interior requires a specific, non-standard layout, custom sizing is always an option. We can adjust the dimensions to fit your architectural constraints while preserving the harmony of the image.

Technical Note on Scale: To guarantee museum-grade sharpness, rich tonal transitions, and uncompromising quality, most photographs in this collection can be flawlessly printed up to 100 cm (approx. 40″) on the longer side.

💡 Framing Tip

For a truly timeless, exhibition-grade look, always prefer framing with anti-reflective museum glass and an acid-free mat. The mat gives the photograph space to breathe, while the glass eliminates distracting reflections, allowing the true colors and textures of the print to shine through.
