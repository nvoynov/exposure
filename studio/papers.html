The Art of Print: Paper

📜 Paper Options & Textures

The choice of paper is the final step in shaping the photograph’s character. We use exclusively museum-grade, acid-free papers from Hahnemühle to ensure breathtaking depth, rich contrast, and a lifespan that spans generations.

🏛️ Matte Finishes (For Depth & Softness)

  • Hahnemühle Photo Rag® (308 gsm)
    • The Fine Art Icon: 100% cotton paper with a smooth, chalky, ultra-matte surface. It feels organic to the touch and completely eliminates reflections. Shadows become deep and velvety, making it perfect for intimate portraits, quiet landscapes, and minimalist compositions.
  • Hahnemühle German Etching (310 gsm)
    • The Sculptural Texture: A heavyweight matte paper with a distinct, fine-grained texture reminiscent of traditional artist’s mold-made paper. It adds a subtle three-dimensional weight to the image, making it an exceptional choice for architectural lines, high-contrast black-and-white work, and rich, painterly textures.

🌟 Glossy Finish (For Radiance & Contrast)

  • Hahnemühle Photo Rag® Baryta (315 gsm)
    • The Noble Radiance: This is not the cheap, artificial gloss of commercial photo paper. It features a sophisticated, subtle luster inherited from classic darkroom baryta prints. It delivers unmatched dynamic range, piercing highlights, and an incredibly wide color gamut that makes vibrant tones pop.

🖼️ Presentation Mechanics: Glass vs. Mounting

Your choice of paper directly influences how the final piece should be framed and displayed.

The Matte Path: Museum Glass

Matte papers (Photo Rag® and German Etching) thrive behind glass. Because the paper itself does not reflect light, pairing it with anti-reflective museum glass creates an invisible barrier. The photograph breathes, and you see only the pure texture and color.

The Baryta Path: Rigid Mounting (Dibond)

Placing a glossy paper like Photo Rag® Baryta behind glass is rarely recommended. The double reflection from both the glass and the paper’s noble luster can conflict, muddying the image. Instead, Baryta reveals its true mastery when left uncovered.

For larger formats, where large-scale passe-partout and museum glass become exceptionally expensive, Photo Rag® Baryta mounted onto a rigid aluminum backing (Dibond) is the ultimate turnkey solution. It offers a sleek, modern, borderless look that sits flush against the wall, cutting framing costs while preserving an uncompromisingly premium presentation.

🧼 Print Care & Longevity

Museum-grade fine art prints are built to last for generations, but their organic cotton surfaces remain delicate and sensitive to the environment. To ensure your print retains its original color depth and texture for decades, please observe these simple handling and placement guidelines.

🧤 Handling the Print

  • The Cotton Rule: Never touch the printed surface with bare hands. Skin oils can leave permanent marks. Always wear clean, lint-free white cotton gloves when handling unframed prints.
  • Edge to Edge: Lift the paper only by its blank outer edges or corners. Never allow the paper to bend or crease, as the structural fibers can break, leaving visible lines.
  • Keep it Covered: Leave the print in its protective archival sleeve or glassine paper until the exact moment it is placed into a frame or mounted onto Dibond.

☀️ Environment & Placement

  • The Shadow Path: Avoid hanging your artwork in areas with prolonged, direct sunlight. Even with UV-protective glass, constant direct sunlight accelerates the aging process. Intense, indirect natural light or dedicated gallery LEDs are ideal.
  • Humidity & Climate: Do not display unframed prints in spaces with high humidity fluctuations, such as bathrooms, kitchens, or directly above radiators and humidifiers. Sudden shifts can cause the organic paper fibers to warp or ripple.

🧽 Cleaning & Maintenance

  • Framed Art: Clean the outer glass using a microfiber cloth slightly dampened with a specialized glass cleaner. Never spray liquid directly onto the frame, as moisture can seep into the mat and damage the paper edges.
  • Mounted Prints (Dibond / Uncovered): To remove dust from an uncovered matte or Baryta print, use an ultra-soft, static-free goat-hair brush or an air blower. Never use water, damp cloths, or commercial detergents on the bare paper surface.
