Figure and Ground

An air raid siren. An air defense salvo, echoed a thousand times over by the wailing of car alarms. More civilian casualties. People snatched from the streets, mobilized for a war for freedom. Disaster, the new normal, expands, devouring and dissolving any sense of “I.”

A few square kilometers of woods near my house. It was always there, but never as a destination—more like a backdrop for rare walks. When the war started, the military took the forest, fencing it off with barbed wire. Now it feels strange that they even gave it back… The previous controllers were chewed up and spat out, and they never found new ones.

Every day brings casualties. A grim winter forecast. There is no good news for you, but a miracle is bound to happen. To fight and die for the motherland is your only and best choice. If you don’t understand this yourself, or worse, if you disagree—we will catch you, break you, and teach you how to love the Motherland the right way. The outside world is compressed and packed into the borders of a single neighborhood. Beyond it lies a checkpoint of the freedom to die.

If only I could escape to the sea or the mountains, for a couple of weeks or more… Instead, I go for a second round, then a third. Spring, a flash of summer, autumn, and winter again. Through the cemetery to the lake. The camera is just an excuse to make another small circle. I’ve lost count. And suddenly, you are inside the scene: observing, sinking deeper. The scene stares back at you. Sometimes it lets you in and allows you to dissolve, gifting a new photograph, or, on rare occasions, an insight.

The war scaled up this familiar patch of land, turning it into a world of the flip side. Imposed heroism turned out to be humanity’s anti-pattern. A byproduct of this shift, where the background became the figure, is this series of images—my first photo project. It is alive: the walks continue, the frames latch onto each other through plot, weather, or mood. Some disappear, others return when a pair is found, or evolve into a new series. The project demanded a website. The website demanded other photographs.
